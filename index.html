FAQ

Direct answers for project planning.

Essential operational criteria for on-site mobilisation, utility setup, testing, and delivery.

MLR Infra manufactures solid concrete bricks, fly ash bricks, interlocking blocks, paver blocks, and precast fencing solutions. The company also provides custom-sized concrete products based on project drawings and specifications.

MLR Infra offers three types of concrete blocks designed for strength, sustainability, and faster construction:

  • Solid Concrete Blocks: Provide high strength and durability, suitable for load-bearing walls, foundations, and other structural masonry applications.
  • Fly Ash Blocks: Concrete building products made using fly ash, offering a more sustainable construction option while maintaining strength.
  • Interlocking Concrete Blocks: Designed with specially shaped edges to fit together without requiring conventional mortar between blocks, helping reduce construction time, labour requirements, and material costs.

MLR Infra offers concrete blocks in five standard sizes (mm) to meet different construction requirements:

  • 300 × 200 × 200 mm
  • 300 × 200 × 150 mm
  • 300 × 200 × 100 mm
  • 290 × 225 × 140 mm
  • 290 × 140 × 100 mm

Custom-Sized Concrete Blocks: Yes, MLR Infra manufactures concrete blocks in custom dimensions based on customer drawings and project specifications. You can contact MLR Infra through the website’s Contact section and share your required dimensions or technical drawings to request a custom-size solution.

Concrete paver blocks are used for driveways, walkways, and outdoor flooring. They are designed to handle vehicle loads and pedestrian traffic without easily cracking or shifting when properly installed.

Vehicle Parking Areas: Yes. Concrete paver blocks are suitable for driveways and vehicle-use areas, depending on the block specifications and installation requirements.

Precast concrete fencing is a modular RCC fencing solution manufactured in advance and assembled at the required site.

  • Types Offered: MLR Infra offers compound walls, cement fencing, RCC fencing, and precast compound wall solutions.
  • Height: MLR Infra provides modular RCC precast fencing solutions up to 6.6 feet in height.

On-site equipment and crew mobilisation is generally viable for projects requiring 30,000 to 50,000+ CC bricks, 1,000+ running meters of precast fencing, or 5,000+ sq meters of paver blocks (smaller orders can be supplied directly from our regional production facilities). An on-site yard requires:

  • Space: Flat, compacted yard area of approx. 8,000 to 15,000 sq ft for casting machinery, raw materials, and curing stacks.
  • Water & Power: Continuous water supply and a 3-phase commercial electric connection (approx. 25–40 HP) or diesel generator space.
  • Access: Clear heavy vehicle access for tippers and bulk cement delivery trucks.
