FAQ

Direct answers for project planning.

Essential operational criteria for on-site mobilisation, utility setup, testing, and delivery.

On-site equipment and crew mobilisation is generally viable for projects requiring 30,000 to 50,000+ CC bricks, 1,000+ running meters of precast fencing, or 5,000+ sq meters of paver blocks. For requirements below this threshold, materials can be supplied directly from our nearest operational regional manufacturing facility.

To establish an on-site mobile manufacturing yard, your site needs:

  • Space: Flat, compacted yard area of approximately 8,000 to 15,000 sq ft for casting machinery, raw material aggregates, and curing stacks.
  • Water: Continuous water supply for concrete batching and systematic curing.
  • Power: 3-phase commercial electric connection (approx. 25–40 HP depending on setup) or designated space for a dedicated diesel generator.
  • Access: Clear, unhindered heavy vehicle access for tippers and bulk cement delivery trucks.

If your volume is below the mobilisation threshold or your site cannot accommodate a mobile setup, you can still source materials through MLR Infra. We supply precast compound walls, CC bricks, and paver blocks directly from our established regional production hubs across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and nearby hubs, palletised and delivered by road transport.

Yes. We cast directly to architectural drawings. Custom moulds for bespoke brick dimensions, specific wall thicknesses (100mm, 150mm, 200mm, 225mm), interlocking profiles, heavy-duty paver shapes (80mm/100mm), and boundary fence heights (up to 6.6 ft, 8 ft, or 10 ft with razor wire posts) can be fabricated and deployed to your project.

Once site feasibility is confirmed and scope is agreed, mobilisation follows a predictable timeline:

  • Setup (Days 1–7): Machine transit, staging bed assembly, electrical and water connections, and raw material aggregate staging.
  • Commissioning (Days 8–12): Test batching, calibration of mix proportions, and operator crew briefing.
  • Continuous Production: Steady daily output (up to 4,000+ units/day) with batch curing scheduled to match your construction consumption rate.

Every on-site casting batch undergoes strict quality control, including aggregate sieve analysis, slump testing, dimension tolerance checks, and 7-day and 28-day concrete cube compressive strength tests. Formal quality inspection logs and test reports are submitted directly to your project QA/QC team.
